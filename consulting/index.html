How I work

Process first, AI second. The technology is the last decision I make, not the first.

Most businesses that come to me have already been sold a tool. The tool is rarely the problem. The problem is a process nobody has looked at closely: work that gets entered twice, a follow-up that depends on somebody remembering, a list of past customers that nobody has spoken to in two years.

So I start there. I find the process that is measurably costing time or revenue, I confirm with you that it is genuinely worth solving rather than merely annoying, and only then do I design something. Sometimes that involves AI. Sometimes it is a much duller fix that works better, and I will tell you when that is the case.

What I build

Systems that do the work a person is currently doing by hand, inside the tools you already pay for. Reactivating customers you have stopped talking to. Answering enquiries in seconds instead of hours. Getting a quote out the same day. Putting the numbers you actually decide on in one place, current, without anyone assembling a spreadsheet.

I build in your stack and I hand it over documented, because a system only you can run is a system I have failed to deliver.

Track record

I have built more than 100 systems for over 20 businesses, from sole traders to companies running teams in the field. I take on a small number of engagements at a time and I answer within 24 hours, because the work needs my attention rather than a queue position.
