I build AI systems for businesses.

Engineer by training, consultant by trade. I find the process that is quietly costing a business time or revenue, and I build the system that fixes it. This site is where I think about that work out loud.

I came to this from reliability engineering, which is the discipline of working out how complex systems fail before they do. It turns out businesses fail in the same shapes machines do: a step nobody owns, a handover where information falls on the floor, a queue that only moves when someone remembers to look at it. Most of what I build is aimed at those seams rather than at anything clever.

Writing here does three things for me. It forces me to understand an idea well enough to put it in order. It keeps an honest record of the journey, including the parts that did not work. And it is a form of public accountability, which I find I need.
